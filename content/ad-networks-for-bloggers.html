How a blog moves up the ladder

A new blog can apply to Google AdSense or Adsterra on day one, because neither states a traffic minimum. Once the blog reaches 10K pageviews a month, Monumetric Propel opens up. Above 250,000 monthly active users, Ezoic is the network built for that scale.

Infolinks sits beside the ladder rather than on it. Its in-text and in-screen formats work as an add-on to a main network, and the site is reviewed by hand.

Traffic and platform rules to check first

  • Monumetric Propel counts pageviews and visitor location. At least half of the traffic must come from the US, UK, Canada, or Australia.
  • Ezoic lets publishers verify traffic by connecting Google Analytics during the application.
  • Infolinks reviews every site and does not accept online gambling or illegal content.

How bloggers get paid

  • Adsterra: from $5 (Paxum, WM), twice a month.
  • Monumetric: $10 threshold, Net 60.
  • Ezoic: $20 threshold, monthly from the 27th.
  • Infolinks: $50 (PayPal, eCheck, ACH), monthly.
  • Google AdSense: $100 threshold, paid between the 21st and the 26th.

Adsterra has the quickest cycle, with two payments a month. Monumetric's Net 60 is the longest default wait of the five.
