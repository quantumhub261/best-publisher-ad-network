Who is connected

This site has a commercial connection with Yango Ads and Yandex Advertising Network. Both appear in the mobile apps group of the comparisons.

How they are treated in the comparisons

Both networks are held to the same inclusion rule as every other network: at least two of three points (entry requirements, ad formats, payout terms) stated on official pages. The rule and the networks left out are on the {{a:methodology|methodology page}}. A commercial connection does not change a network's figures, the verdict criteria, or its place in a table.
