Quick verdicts

  • Broad demand through one SDK: Yango Ads.
  • One SDK with GDPR consent support: Yandex Advertising Network.
  • The baseline, with mediation and bidding: Google AdMob.
  • Unity games that need mediation: Unity LevelPlay.
  • The most flexible payouts: Appodeal.

What an app needs from an ad network

  • SDK platforms. All five list Android, iOS, Unity, and Flutter. Yango Ads, Yandex Advertising Network, Unity LevelPlay, and Appodeal also list React Native.
  • Formats. Rewarded, interstitial, and banner ads appear in every list. The network whose formats match the app's ad placements is the better fit.
  • Payout terms. Thresholds run from $100 (Google AdMob, and Appodeal through PayPal or ACH) to $150 or €100 (Yango Ads and Yandex Advertising Network). Unity LevelPlay does not publish its terms on the pages checked.
  • Mediation. Google AdMob and Unity LevelPlay offer it, and Appodeal is built around it.

How to choose an in-app ad network

  1. Match the SDK to the engine: native Android or iOS, Unity, Flutter, or React Native.
  2. Check the format list against the ad placements. Games usually lean on rewarded and interstitial ads.
  3. Compare the payout threshold with expected monthly revenue. Earnings below the threshold wait until the balance reaches it.
  4. Read the store and audience rules. Yandex Advertising Network, for example, requires the app to be live in the App Store or Google Play.
