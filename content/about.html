What this site does

{{site.name}} compares ad networks for publishers in the US market. It covers two groups, networks for websites and networks for mobile apps. Each comparison shows entry requirements, ad formats, and payout terms, gives a verdict, and links to the page each figure came from.

Who it is for

Site owners and bloggers choosing a first or second ad network, and app developers choosing an SDK to monetize with.

How it stays current

Figures are dated and rechecked against the networks' own pages. The rules are on the {{a:methodology|methodology page}}, and the commercial connections are on the {{a:disclosure|disclosure page}}.
