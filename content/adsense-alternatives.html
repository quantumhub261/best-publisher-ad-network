AdSense terms for reference

Google AdSense states no traffic minimum. It asks that publishers be 18 or older, have original content, and have access to the site's HTML. It pays once earnings reach $100, between the 21st and the 26th of the month, and offers display, in-feed, in-article, and multiplex units plus site search.

When AdSense is not the best fit

  • The site needs other formats. Ezoic lists sticky video, anchor, rewarded, and interstitial. Adsterra lists Popunder and Social Bar. Infolinks lists in-text and in-screen units.
  • The site needs a lower payout threshold. Adsterra, Monumetric, Ezoic, and Infolinks all state thresholds below AdSense's $100.
  • The traffic is already large. Ezoic is built for sites with 250,000+ monthly active users.

Before switching

  1. Check the entry requirements first. Several alternatives screen sites by hand.
  2. Read the network's policy on running other ad networks on the same pages.
  3. Compare payout terms, not only thresholds. Monumetric pays on Net 60, while Ezoic defaults to Net 30.
